# Profile kit — @mindblownreels

Everything Instagram will not let the pipeline change for you. It takes about 15 minutes on
your phone. Do it once; the series only start paying off when a profile visit turns into a follow.

Generated picture: `profile-pic.png` (1080×1080). The small preview `profile-pic-small.png`
is what it looks like in a comment — `WHY?` still reads, which is the only thing that matters.

---

## 1. Name field (30 characters — keywords are searchable)

```
Everyday Why | Hidden Facts
```

The name is not the username. `@mindblownreels` stays; this line is what people can search.
Do not put "Reels" or "Daily" here — nobody searches for those.

## 2. Bio (150 characters)

```
The hidden reason behind everyday things.
New Reel daily. Made with AI.
👇 Start with the pinned ones
```

Rules for the bio: one promise, one cadence, no hashtags, no emojis beyond the arrow.
It must answer "what do I get if I follow" in under three seconds.

## 3. Profile picture

Upload `profile-pic.png`. It is amber `#FFD60A` with black `WHY?`, the same amber that
the Reels already use for the highlighted word. That is deliberate: the feed, the covers
and the avatar look like one account instead of three.

## 4. Category and contact

- Category: **Digital creator**
- Threads: on (same handle) — free distribution, no extra work
- Contact: leave blank

## 5. Highlights (5, in this order)

Create them empty now and fill as Reels go out. Each one is a reason to stay on the profile.

| Highlight | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| Objects | The Hidden Feature episodes |
| Food | The Label Decoder episodes about food |
| Labels | The Label Decoder episodes about products |
| Why Not | Why Is There No… episodes |
| Fails | That Shouldn't Work episodes |

Use the cover from the Reel itself as the highlight cover, cropped to the same amber/black
style. No custom art.

## 6. Pin these three (already posted)

In this order, best first:

1. **Why the Amazon River Has No Bridges** — 5 of the account's 8 total shares. The proof that
   familiar subject + unfamiliar detail works.
2. **Squirrel Gravity Defiance** — 2 shares, highest reach of the animal posts.
3. **The Floating Paperclip** — highest views (382), zero shares. Pin it third, not first: it
   pulls views but it does not prove the promise.

Unpin everything else. Three pins are the shop window.

## 7. First nine covers

The grid is read as one picture before anyone reads a caption. For the next nine Reels:

- same font, same amber highlight word, same black band at the bottom
- 2 or 3 words only (`*WATCH* POCKET`, `*NO* WINDOW`)
- one clear subject, no faces, no text on the photo
- alternate close-up and wide so the grid has rhythm

The pipeline already writes `cover_text` in that style and saves `cover-<id>.jpg` with every
render. Post with the cover the render produced; do not design a new one per Reel.

## 8. Series in the caption

Every caption opens with the search phrase, and the series name goes at the end of the first
paragraph as an episode mark:

```
The Hidden Feature #3
```

That is what makes a new viewer open the profile: they see there are more of the same thing,
not more random facts.

## 9. Where the follow comes from

At ~140 reach per Reel, a 1% follow rate is 1.4 followers. You are at 0.03% because the
profile gives a visitor no next step. The kit above fixes the three things a visitor checks:
the picture, the name, and the first three Reels.

Track only this: **profile visits → follows**. It is in Instagram Insights under
"Profile activity". If visits rise but follows do not, the bio is the problem, not the Reels.
